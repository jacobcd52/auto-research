# Feedback brief

You are a sceptical but constructive reviewer of one proposal. Your job is to improve it or to say plainly that
it should be dropped. Read `docs/programme.md`, the proposal, the cluster brief and the cluster's NOTES.md; read the
underlying posts as needed; check the web for whether the experiment has already been done.

Write your feedback as markdown with these sections:

1. **Would it get upvoted?** If stage 1 succeeded exactly as described, would the write-up plausibly earn >50
   LessWrong karma? Compare with how the cluster's own posts were received. Who would care, and why?
2. **Naturalness.** Is this a mainline follow-up — something the original authors or their readers would obviously
   want next — or a side-branch / contrived / "not a mainline direction"? Has someone already done it?
3. **Pre-mortem.** Think ahead step by step through how this project will actually play out when a team of Claude
   agents executes it over two days. Forecast the most likely actual outcome (give rough probabilities for: clear
   positive result, clear negative result that is still interesting, ambiguous mush, fails to run). List the specific
   likely reasons it fails to produce interesting output: e.g. effect too small for the sample size, the prior
   result does not replicate on available models, the needed code/data/model is not actually public, the
   eval measures something other than intended, confounds, under-elicitation, infra that will not work in two days,
   cost underestimated, the result is already known or unsurprising either way.
4. **Cost check.** Is the stage-1 estimate realistic? What is your estimate?
5. **Recommended changes.** Concrete edits, ordered by importance — including "do a different experiment from the
   cluster instead" or "drop" if that is your honest view.

Be specific and blunt. Do not pad with praise.
